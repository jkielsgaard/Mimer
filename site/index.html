How it works

Write. Organise. Ask. Keep.

01 / WRITE

Plain Markdown

A fast editor that shows your formatting as you type. Every note is an ordinary .md file in an ordinary folder.

02 / ORGANISE

Tasks and meetings

Tasks live in your notes. Meetings have an agenda, a place to write while you talk, and the tasks that came out of them.

03 / ASK

An AI that suggests

Fix, rewrite or find things with a model on your own computer. It proposes; you read the change and accept it.

04 / KEEP

Nothing is lost

Every change is saved in a built-in Git history. Bring back any version of a note, or a note you deleted.

Features

Everything in one place, nothing locked in

01 / Tasks

Tasks that live in your notes

A task is a checklist line, so it works in any editor. Group them into epics, fold what you are not working on, and focus on one thing at a time.

  • Epics with steps, a note and a log of what happened
  • A status label on every task: Doing, Waiting or Dropped
  • Focus mode with an optional timer, or just the one task
How tasks work →
02 / AI

An AI that only suggests

Select text and choose what to do. Mimer shows the change next to the original, and nothing is written until you accept it.

  • Runs on your computer: built-in models, or LM Studio
  • Fixed commands that do one thing and return only the result
  • Works in every text box, not only in notes
What stays on your computer →
03 / Meetings and the start page

From agenda to action

Prepare a meeting, run it from one calm screen, and turn what was said into tasks. Your start page shows what you need today, built from widgets you choose.

  • A calendar of meetings and a screen to write in while you talk
  • Next meetings, recent notes and pinned notes at a glance
  • Write down a task, an idea or a quick note from anywhere
04 / Your look

Eight themes, one for every desk

Pick the look that is easy on your eyes: light, dark, or one of six calm palettes. The choice is shown live, and Mimer can follow your computer's light or dark setting.

  • Slate, Paper, Mint, Midnight, Graphite and Ember
  • Readable contrast in every theme
  • Your notes never change: the theme is only how Mimer looks

Privacy

Your notes stay on your computer

No account, no tracking

Mimer does not phone home. There is nothing to sign up for.

A local AI by default

The AI reads your notes only on your own machine. A cloud AI is possible, but only after you say yes, and never in a project you lock to local.

You decide what is written

The AI never changes a note on its own. Every change is a proposal you review.
