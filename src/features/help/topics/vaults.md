## Several vaults

Keep things apart by using more than one vault, for example one for work and one private. Each vault has its own notes, tabs, search, chat history and version history.

## Switch

Click the **vault name** at the top, or press **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+O**. Choose another vault from the list. The one that is open has a check mark.

- **Open another folder as vault…** adds an existing folder.
- **Create new vault…** makes a new folder with the standard folders and templates.

When you switch, the open note is saved and the old vault is committed first. When you come back, its tabs are where you left them.

## Good to know

- A vault is just a folder. You can open it in other editors or back it up by copying it.
- Settings such as the AI providers are shared by all vaults. Settings under **Vault** belong to one vault.
