# VPN rollout plan

We roll out the new VPN client to all laptops before the end of November.

## Goals

- Everyone is on the new client by 30 November.
- The old client is removed afterwards.
- Support has a short guide to send to users.

## Risks

| Risk | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Laptops that are rarely online | Offer a drop-in hour |
| Missing licences | Order 20 extra |
